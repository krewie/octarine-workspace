# 存　ソン　ゾン

exist, suppose, be aware of, believe

Core idea: to exist / to remain / to be aware

| Word | 読み | 意味 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 存在 | そんさい | existence, to exist |
| 保存 | ほぞん | preservation, saving |
| 生存 | せいぞん | survival |
| ご存じ | ごぞんじ | to know, to be aware |
| 存じる | ぞんじる | to know / think, humble language |
| 所存 | しょぞん | intention, what one intends to |

Examples

> その問題は存在します。
>
> That problem exists.

> このファイルを保存します。
>
> I will save this file.

> 田中さんをご存じですか。
>
> Do you know Mr Tanaka ?

Important 存 itself does not directly mean “believe”.
That meaning comes from expressions such as

> そう存じます。
>
> I believe so / think so.

Here, 存じます is the humble form of 思います。

# 倉 

**On-reading:** ソウ
**Kun-reading:** くら

Meaning: **warehouse / storehouse**

| てんご | 読み | 意味 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 倉 | くら | warehouse, storehouse |
| 倉庫 | そうこ | warehouse |
| 船倉 | せんそう | ships hold |
| 米倉 | こめぐら | rice storehouse |

Examples

> 商品は倉庫にあります。　しょうひん (goods, commodities)
>
> The goods are in the warehouse.

- 倉 is the actual storage building.
- 倉庫 is the more common modern word for warehouse.
